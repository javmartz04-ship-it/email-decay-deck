Part 2 · The good news

We fix all of this with two things.

Each one takes under 30 minutes. You'll see the difference in your opens and clicks within a week.

1Re-engage the quiet ones
2Remove whoever doesn't answer
1

Thing 1

Run a re‑engagement campaign.

Three short emails to everyone who hasn't opened or clicked in 90 days. One question:

"Hey, do you still want
to hear from me?"

To: inactive · 90 days
1
We miss you

Still want these? Click here and you stay on the list.

2
Last chance

Haven't heard from you. One click keeps you subscribed.

3
Final notice

I'm taking you off the list. No hard feelings.

2

Thing 2

Sunset the non‑responders.

Didn't respond to any of the three? Remove them.

Not "maybe later." Not a separate list you keep emailing. Gone.
